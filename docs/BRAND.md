# Mazzone Olive Oil — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence
**Tagline**: Dal cuore della Sicilia, alla tua tavola.
*(From the heart of Sicily, to your table.)*

## Brand Pillars
1. **Heritage** — Multi-generational family tradition
2. **Terroir** — Sicilian land, climate, and varietals
3. **Craft** — Small-batch, hand-harvested, cold-pressed
4. **Modern Luxury** — Elevated everyday experience

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette
| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Olive Dark | `#2D4A2D` | Primary, headings, nav |
| Olive | `#4A7C4A` | Accents, buttons |
| Olive Light | `#8BAF8B` | Hover states, secondary |
| Gold | `#C4963C` | Premium accents, CTAs |
| Gold Light | `#E8D5A3` | Highlights, badges |
| Terracotta | `#C4603C` | Warm accents, alerts |
| Cream | `#FDF8F0` | Backgrounds |
| Warm White | `#FEFCF9` | Card backgrounds |
| Charcoal | `#2D2D2D` | Body text |
| Stone | `#6B6B6B` | Secondary text |

### Typography
- **Headings**: Playfair Display (serif) — elegant, editorial
- **Body**: Inter (sans-serif) — clean, readable
- **Accents/Italian phrases**: Playfair Display Italic

### Photography Style
- Warm, golden-hour lighting
- Rustic textures: aged wood, terracotta, linen
- Close-ups of oil (golden-green pour shots)
- Sicilian landscapes: groves, stone walls, Mediterranean sea
- Family/artisanal process: hands harvesting, pressing

### Design Principles
- **Whitespace**: Let content breathe, luxury feel
- **Contrast**: Dark olive on cream, gold accents sparingly
- **Motion**: Subtle parallax, fade-ins, no flashy animations
- **Layout**: Asymmetric grids, full-bleed imagery, editorial feel

## Voice & Tone
- Warm, inviting, knowledgeable
- Mix Italian and English naturally (Italian for emotional/cultural, English for practical)
- Never salesy — confident, understated
- Storytelling over features

## Key Italian Phrases
- "Dal cuore della Sicilia" — From the heart of Sicily
- "Tre generazioni di tradizione" — Three generations of tradition
- "Fatto con amore" — Made with love
- "La dolce vita quotidiana" — The sweet daily life
- "Dalla terra alla tavola" — From the land to the table
- "Olio nuovo" — New oil (first press of the season)
- "Spremitura a freddo" — Cold-pressed

## Product Categories
1. **Olio Extra Vergine** — Core single-origin and blended oils
2. **Olio Nuovo** — Seasonal, limited first-press
3. **Infusi** — Infused oils (lemon, chili, garlic)
4. **Aceto** — Aged vinegars
5. **Regali** — Gift sets and curated boxes
6. **Il Club** — Subscription olive oil club

## Competitors & Positioning
- **Above**: Grocery store olive oil (Bertolli, Colavita)
- **Alongside**: Brightland, Graza, Fat Gold
- **Differentiation**: Authentic Sicilian single-origin, family heritage (not rebranded commodity oil)
