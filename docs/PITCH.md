# Mazzone Olive Oil — Digital Elevation Pitch

## The Opportunity

Mazzone Olive Oil has an authentic story, real Sicilian heritage, and quality product.
Their current WordPress/Divi site doesn't match the premium positioning their product deserves.

**We're proposing a complete digital transformation** that positions Mazzone alongside
modern DTC olive oil brands (Brightland, Graza) while leaning into their authentic
Italian heritage as the key differentiator.

## What We're Building

### 1. Brand-First E-Commerce Experience
- Modern, editorial design that feels like an Italian lifestyle magazine
- Product pages that tell the story of each oil (origin, harvest, tasting notes)
- Seamless checkout with Stripe
- Mobile-first responsive design

### 2. Subscription Revenue (Il Club Mazzone)
- Monthly or quarterly olive oil club
- Curated selections with tasting cards
- Predictable recurring revenue
- Community building

### 3. Content & SEO Engine
- Recipe blog with beautiful photography integration
- Pairing guides (which oil with which dish)
- Farm life / harvest season updates
- Cultural stories connecting food to heritage

### 4. Operational Upgrade
- Real-time inventory management
- Order fulfillment tracking
- Low-stock alerts
- Customer relationship management

### 5. Growth Infrastructure
- Email marketing (newsletter + automation)
- Analytics and conversion tracking
- SEO-optimized architecture
- Social proof (reviews, Instagram integration)

## Technical Stack
| Layer | Choice | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| Frontend | Next.js on Vercel | Blazing fast, SEO-perfect, scales infinitely |
| Backend | FastAPI (Python) | Modern, fast, easy to maintain |
| Database | Supabase | Auth, storage, realtime — all-in-one |
| Payments | Stripe | Industry standard, subscriptions built-in |
| Email | Resend | Beautiful transactional emails |
